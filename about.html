Curation

The archive is editorially guided. A show can be indexed before a long review exists if it is useful to the catalog and has enough verified metadata to recommend responsibly.

Ratings

Archive Rating is the site's editorial stance. Community Rating is anonymous listener input and stays hidden until at least five ratings exist, so tiny samples do not pretend to be consensus.

Scope

The Echo Archives is not a playback app or a social network. It is a curated discovery archive for audio dramas and fiction podcasts.
