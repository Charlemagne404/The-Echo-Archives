Review notes

The pitch and the reaction, without burying the page in old inline styling.

Summary

Impact Winter is a post-apocalyptic audio drama set seven years after a comet slammed into Earth, plunging the world into a devastating winter and endless darkness. But that was not the only nightmare unleashed. From the shadows came bloodthirsty monsters. Vampires. The year is 2042. Darci and her younger sister Hope live in a bunker beneath a medieval castle, surviving alongside a small group of others while they wait for the sun to return. But in this brutal new world, nothing is as it seems, including the monsters everyone thinks they understand.

Own thoughts

I absolutely loved Impact Winter. It grabs you by the throat and pulls you in. Season 1 is the high point, and it eases off a bit by season 3, but overall it is still an easy recommendation if you want a polished, aggressive audio drama.
