Summary

OZ 9 is a space comedy following the wildly incompetent crew of a spaceship called the Oz 9. Built by a shady corporation named Gated Galaxies, the ship was supposedly designed to transport rich people across the universe to colonize new planets, but it becomes clear quickly that that is not its only purpose. The crew are disasters, the AI is deeply unhelpful, and most of the fun comes from watching the whole thing keep getting worse in exactly the dumbest way possible.

Own thoughts

I was not the biggest fan of OZ 9 at first. It takes a while to get going, and I usually prefer more serious audio dramas. This is the exact opposite of that. It gets more interesting after the first season, and I get why some people really love it. Not really for me, but not bad either.
