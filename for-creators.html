Editorial standards

These are the rules behind verification, corrections, and archive trust.

Read the full standards page

Accuracy first

Factual metadata, links, and release details must be reviewable against credible sources.

Transparency

Verification and correction work is about facts, not hidden influence over archive opinions.

Respect and inclusion

Submissions should improve the archive without harassment, misinformation, or abusive behavior.

Clear listener expectations

Descriptions, tags, and format details should help listeners know what kind of experience they are choosing.

Rights and originality

Archive records should respect copyright, creator attribution, and the factual boundary between official and editorial material.

Ongoing upkeep

Metadata can be refreshed over time, but nothing skips review and no paid path overrides archive standards.
