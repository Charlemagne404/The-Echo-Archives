Essential and optional use

What kind of storage this is

The current storage items are functional or security-related. They support chat and scroll continuity, publicly anonymous ratings, Cloudflare challenge protection, and maintainer authentication. The community voter storage is technically necessary for the rating or helpful-vote functionality; first-party analytics storage is only for aggregate operational measurement. The protected report can group trends, top pages, shows, collections, discovery surfaces, and conversion stages for a selected range, but it does not expose an individual browsing history. The maintainer analytics page does not collect its own browser analytics, and its report data is available only after maintainer authentication.

The archive does not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics cookies, or cross-site marketing cookies. Public pages do use the two first-party browser-storage keys listed above for aggregate operational analytics; they do not hold raw IPs, raw user agents, or a fingerprint on the server. No separate preference-cookie layer is implemented today. Cloudflare may inject RUM at the edge as a performance-measurement service; this page does not assert its storage or retention behavior without current provider and production evidence. Cloudflare separately processes request and security data and may set challenge-related storage as described in the inventory above.

Community ratings

What happens on show pages

Opening a show page reads the public rating summary and can read an existing rating profile, but it does not create a profile or set the archive’s voter cookie. The publicly anonymous, pseudonymous rating identifiers are created only when you submit or clear a score or mark a listener review helpful.

In practical terms, the rating system currently uses both the HTTP-only voter cookie and the linked local profile id. The cookie is not readable from frontend JavaScript, while the local profile id is used by the browser-side rating UI.

For the current implementation, the community identifiers support rating or helpful-vote actions after you actively request them. The separate analytics identifiers support aggregate operational measurement on public pages and are not reused for ratings, advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking. Blocking or clearing browser storage can make visitor, session, and conversion counts less complete; page-view counts can still be recorded without a durable browser token.

Your controls

How to clear or limit storage

  • Clearing site data in your browser removes session storage, localStorage, and first-party cookies for the archive.
  • Blocking the archive’s cookies can interfere with publicly anonymous community rating behavior; blocking browser storage reduces the accuracy of analytics visitor and session estimates.
  • Logging out of protected maintainer routes clears the maintainer session cookie.
Future changes

What happens if storage changes later

If the archive adds advertising, third-party analytics, or preference storage later, this page should be updated with the purpose, duration, and whether that storage is essential or optional. The privacy page should be updated at the same time.

The current implementation does not add a site-wide cookie banner for advertising or third-party marketing cookies. The first-party analytics storage is documented in the inventory above. If the storage model or legal basis changes, this page and the consent flow must be updated before deployment.
