The problem with podcast discovery

Most podcast apps are built to keep you scrolling, not help you decide what story is worth your next hour.

  • Popularity gets rewarded over quality. Big audiences and launch momentum can matter more than how good a show is.
  • Trending charts do not understand mood. They can show what is popular, but not whether it matches the tone, pace, or time you have tonight.
  • Algorithms optimize engagement. Platforms are built to hold your attention, and paid promotion can push good fiction out of view.

Echo Archives starts with the listener's actual question: what should I listen to next?

What makes Echo Archives different
