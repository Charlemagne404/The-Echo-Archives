Support the archive

Echo Archives is meant to stay free, ad-free, and listener-first. If the archive helps you find your next favorite show, support helps keep the catalog growing without sponsor pressure or paid placement.

Support options

The public support path is Patreon. The archive does not publish supporter names, counts, avatars, or donor tiers on this site.

No placeholder social proof. No paid placement. No sponsor influence on ratings or collection order.

What support pays for

Support keeps the archive stable, current, and worth trusting over time.

  • Hosting, uptime, databases, and day-to-day maintenance
  • Metadata cleanup, corrections, and official link verification
  • Catalog growth, submission review, and new show intake
  • Full reviews, curated collections, and archive notes that improve discovery
  • Long-term upkeep for ratings, search, and archive tools

Listener-supported by design

Echo Archives answers to listeners, not advertisers. Support helps keep recommendations independent, placement unsold, and the archive useful for the long haul.
