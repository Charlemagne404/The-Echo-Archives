Use of the site

You may browse the archive, use discovery tools, and submit corrections, reviews, and verification requests.

You may not use the site to spam, scrape abusively, interfere with service availability, or submit unlawful, deceptive, or malicious material.

Editorial independence

Archive ratings, archive notes, and collection placement are editorial decisions. Creator verification only signals metadata confirmation and does not imply creator approval of ratings, reviews, or curation choices.

  • Money does not buy ratings, reviews, collection placement, or archive recommendations.
  • Creator corrections can improve objective facts, not editorial judgment.
  • The archive may revise copy, labels, and organization as the catalog evolves.

User submissions

By sending a submission, you confirm that the information is accurate to the best of your knowledge and that you have the right to share it.

Submissions are moderated and may be edited, declined, or held for follow-up instead of being published automatically.

Community ratings and reviews

Community ratings and listener reviews are meant to help other listeners make better choices. Do not use them for harassment, brigading, impersonation, spam, or attempts to distort a show record.

The archive may remove or ignore abusive, deceptive, duplicated, or off-topic community input.

Accuracy, links, and rights

The archive aims for high-quality metadata and careful editorial work, but it cannot guarantee that every page is complete, current, or error-free at all times.

The site links to official show pages, listening platforms, and third-party services for convenience. The Echo Archives does not control those services and is not responsible for their content, uptime, or policies.

Unless otherwise noted, the site design, archive copy, and original project materials belong to their respective rights holders. Podcast names, cover art, and related marks remain the property of their owners.
