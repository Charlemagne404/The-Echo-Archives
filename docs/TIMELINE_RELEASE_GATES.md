# Timeline - Release Gates

## Purpose

This document defines the checkpoints that must be true before the archive moves into the next wave of exposure.

## Gate A - Stable Public Beta

Target date:

- June 28, 2026

Required product checks:

- homepage trust signals are live
- Ask the Archivist naming is consistent
- no broken main-route flows remain

Required editorial checks:

- every live show has consistent core metadata
- all featured collections have clear descriptions

Required platform checks:

- submissions and corrections are reaching the intended destination
- sitemap and robots behavior are verified

Promotion action:

- complete soft launch to a trusted small audience

## Gate B - Community Growth Ready

Target date:

- August 23, 2026

Required product checks:

- at least 50 shows are live
- at least 8 full reviews are live
- at least 10 collections are live

Required editorial checks:

- controlled vocab v1 is stable
- recommendation reasons exist for the strongest featured paths

Required platform checks:

- catalog validation is catching bad links and structural mistakes

Promotion action:

- start broader community launch in audio drama spaces

## Gate C - Discovery Advantage Ready

Target date:

- October 18, 2026

Required product checks:

- advanced filters are live
- recently added and recently updated views are live
- search empty states have useful recovery paths

Required editorial checks:

- best-for tags and similar-show links have been cleaned for redundancy

Required platform checks:

- discovery features are driven from structured metadata

Promotion action:

- promote the archive as a real discovery tool, not just a catalog

## Gate D - Trust And Contribution Ready

Target date:

- December 13, 2026

Required product checks:

- archive rating and community rating are clearly distinct
- listener-review and creator-correction paths are understandable

Required editorial checks:

- moderation rules are documented and applied
- creator verification rules are explicit

Required platform checks:

- community average threshold is enforced
- anti-spam protections exist for public contribution endpoints

Promotion action:

- begin creator outreach and invite higher-trust contributions

## Gate E - Final-Vision Release Candidate

Target date:

- April 4, 2027

Required product checks:

- 100 to 125 shows are live
- 20 to 30 full reviews are live
- 15 or more collections are live
- creator and network layers improve browsing where present

Required editorial checks:

- the archive reads as coherent and intentional across old and new entries
- spoiler, rating, and contribution policy language is final

Required platform checks:

- public routes, submission flows, ratings, and sitemap behavior have passed release QA

Promotion action:

- relaunch and position the archive as a durable public discovery product
