# Launch Plan

This doc now supports the unified roadmap rather than acting like a one-time MVP checklist.

For sequencing, use `DEVELOPMENT_TIMELINE.md` and `TIMELINE_RELEASE_GATES.md`.

## Positioning

Primary line:

- Find your next audio obsession.

Supporting line:

- Podcast apps are built for everything. The Echo Archives is built for fiction.

Short product description:

- The Echo Archives is a curated discovery archive for fiction podcasts and audio dramas, organized around listening intent, not generic rankings.

## Launch Waves

### Wave 1 - Soft Launch

Target window:

- June 2026 during Phase 1

Goal:

- fix trust, QA, wording, and metadata issues before wider exposure

### Wave 2 - Community Launch

Target window:

- late July to August 2026 during Phase 2

Goal:

- drive submissions, missing-show discoveries, and early recommendation feedback once the archive reaches stronger catalog depth

### Wave 3 - Creator Outreach

Target window:

- October to December 2026 during Phase 4

Goal:

- improve metadata accuracy, creator verification, and archive credibility

## Messaging Assets

### Site blurb

- The Echo Archives helps listeners find fiction podcasts by taste instead of generic rankings. The catalog is curated, spoiler-safe, and built around mood, tone, format, and listening context.

### Short social post

- The Echo Archives is live. It is a curated discovery archive for fiction podcasts and audio dramas, built around mood, tone, format, and listening intent. Find your next audio obsession: `https://echo.continental-hub.com`

### Creator outreach note

- The Echo Archives is a human-curated discovery archive for fiction podcasts. Your show is part of how we help listeners navigate the space. If you want to correct metadata or add official links, the archive is here: `https://echo.continental-hub.com`

### Community framing

- I am building a useful archive for audio drama listeners, not just a directory. If something is missing, mislabeled, or deserves to be here, submit it and help improve the archive.

## Outreach Priorities

- owned Continental channels
- featured creators already represented in the archive
- listener communities that already discuss audio drama recommendations
- genre-specific spaces for sci-fi, horror, mystery, and comedy fiction podcasts

## Launch Rule

Do not increase promotion faster than the archive can absorb:

- corrections
- submissions
- metadata cleanup
- moderation work

The archive should widen exposure only when trust keeps pace.
