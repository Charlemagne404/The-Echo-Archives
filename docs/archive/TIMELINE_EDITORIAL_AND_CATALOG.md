# Timeline - Editorial And Catalog

## Scope

This document covers the content and curation side of the roadmap:

- catalog growth
- metadata quality
- review throughput
- collection curation
- submission handling
- creator verification

## Editorial Rules

- Metadata quality beats raw volume.
- Indexed entries are permanent first-class records, not placeholders.
- Full reviews should grow steadily, but the archive should never stall while waiting for them.
- If capacity gets tight, prefer more indexed entries plus selective quick takes over fewer polished pages and a stagnant catalog.

## Capacity Assumption

This plan assumes one primary editor-maintainer with occasional outside feedback.

That means the sustainable cadence should be conservative:

- 2 to 4 new indexed shows per week during growth phases
- 1 full review every 1 to 2 weeks
- 1 collection refresh or new collection per week during discovery phases

If available editorial time increases, raise catalog and review targets before adding new feature classes.

## Phase 1 - Stable Public Beta

Metadata work:

- clean inconsistent descriptions, tone labels, and best-for tags
- validate every listen link and image path
- normalize completion and release statuses

Editorial operations:

- lock a v1 review template
- lock a v1 indexed-entry template
- define moderation SLA for corrections and submissions

Definition of done:

- no live entry feels obviously under-described or mislabeled

## Phase 2 - Catalog Depth And Editorial Coverage

Catalog work:

- prioritize breadth across sci-fi, horror, mystery, comedy, and beginner-friendly entries
- avoid overconcentrating on one subgenre

Review work:

- publish 5 to 7 additional full reviews
- introduce quick-take coverage where a full review is not yet realistic

Collection work:

- add beginner-friendly
- add completed shows
- add short commitment
- add strong sound design
- add character-driven recommendations

Definition of done:

- the archive can serve both existing fans and first-time listeners

## Phase 3 - Discovery Upgrade

Metadata work:

- make best-for tags more reliable and less redundant
- add structured recommendation reasons where available
- tighten similar-show relationships into a usable network

Collection work:

- stop treating collections as fixed homepage ornaments
- maintain them as active editorial discovery products

Definition of done:

- collections and similar-show paths reflect actual archive judgment instead of loose tagging

## Phase 4 - Contribution And Moderation Systems

Listener reviews:

- add intake schema
- define spoiler labels
- define moderation standards
- define how much editing is acceptable before publication

Creator verification:

- define what can be verified
- define what cannot be influenced by creators
- store source links or provenance notes for factual corrections

Definition of done:

- outside contributions increase coverage and trust without blurring editorial ownership

## Phase 5 - Creator, Network, And Archive Context Layer

Catalog work:

- add creator records for high-value repeated entities
- add network records where they improve discovery or show relationships

Editorial work:

- pilot creator notes or short Q&A format
- ensure creator content is clearly separated from reviews

Definition of done:

- creator and network layers enrich context rather than adding empty directory pages

## Phase 6 - Final-Vision Release Candidate

Catalog work:

- bring the archive to critical mass
- close obvious genre and tone gaps
- ensure full reviews represent the archive's strongest editorial voice

Editorial work:

- review older entries for consistency as newer standards mature
- remove or rewrite weak summaries that no longer meet quality expectations

Definition of done:

- the archive feels coherent even though not every show has identical depth

## Permanent Quality Checks

Run these checks throughout every phase:

- no broken official or listen links
- no duplicate or near-duplicate taxonomy terms
- no copied marketing blurbs without archive framing
- no community or creator copy presented as archive editorial judgment
- no full-review label without genuinely richer review content
