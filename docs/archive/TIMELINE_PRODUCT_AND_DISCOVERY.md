# Timeline - Product And Discovery

## Scope

This document breaks down the user-facing product work needed in each roadmap phase.

It covers:

- homepage and browse flows
- search and filtering
- show pages
- collection pages
- recommendation paths
- Ask the Archivist UX

## Product Principles

- Every public page should help a listener decide what to hear next.
- Discovery should center listening intent, not only genre labels.
- Indexed-only entries must still feel like valid archive records.
- Empty states should become invitations to browse, compare, or submit.

## Phase 1 - Stable Public Beta

Homepage:

- expose archive stats
- expose last-updated signal
- add a visible archive activity or changelog entry point
- confirm hero copy, CTA hierarchy, and footer language match current branding guidance

Show pages:

- standardize review-status language
- make indexed-only messaging explicit instead of sounding incomplete
- ensure correction CTA is visible without overpowering the main content

Ask the Archivist:

- rename remaining generic "chat" language to "Ask the Archivist"
- tighten suggestion prompts around real archive questions

Definition of done:

- a new visitor can understand the archive within one homepage visit
- a show without a full review still feels intentional

## Phase 2 - Catalog Depth And Editorial Coverage

Homepage:

- promote more than three discovery paths
- rotate featured collections based on editorial intent, not fixed placement forever

Collections:

- expand from 6 to at least 10
- add coverage for beginner-friendly, completed, short-commitment, and highly produced listening paths

Show pages:

- add stronger recommendation blocks
- surface reasons for similar-show links where available

Definition of done:

- the archive can recommend across multiple moods and commitment levels, not just a narrow sci-fi subset

## Phase 3 - Discovery Upgrade

Search:

- support stronger matching across genre, tone, best-for, completion status, and similarity
- make empty states suggest collections, submit flow, or Ask the Archivist prompts

Filtering:

- add completion-status filter
- add review-status filter
- add runtime or commitment filter
- add best-for filter

Browse:

- add recently added view
- add recently updated view
- improve all-collections browsing and collection page depth

Recommendation paths:

- show "If you liked this, try..." reasons on show pages
- make collection inclusion feel editorial rather than arbitrary

Definition of done:

- discovery feels meaningfully better than searching inside a normal podcast app

## Phase 4 - Contribution And Moderation Systems

Show pages:

- separate archive rating, community rating, listener reviews, and creator notes visually and semantically

Submission UX:

- expand submit flow to cover listener reviews and creator verification requests
- make moderation expectations explicit before submission

Trust:

- make community rating thresholds visible in copy
- make verified factual updates distinguishable from editorial content

Definition of done:

- users can contribute without confusing contributions with editorial canon

## Phase 5 - Creator, Network, And Archive Context Layer

Entity browsing:

- add creator pages only when they help discovery
- add network pages only when they clarify relationships between shows

Archive context:

- create a changelog or updates surface that shows archive growth over time
- connect show pages to creator, network, and collection context where it improves recommendation trust

Definition of done:

- the archive feels interconnected rather than like isolated show records

## Phase 6 - Final-Vision Release Candidate

Homepage:

- present the archive as obviously alive, curated, and substantial
- ensure key discovery modes are visible above the fold or one scroll away

Show pages:

- full reviews should support archive take, spoiler-free review, score breakdown, best-for tags, similar shows, and community layer cleanly
- indexed-only pages should still provide enough metadata to justify inclusion

Ask the Archivist:

- recommendations should stay tightly grounded in the catalog
- prompts should reflect mature discovery use cases such as completion status, mood, time commitment, and similar-show comparisons

Definition of done:

- the public product experience matches the promise of `FINAL_VISION.md` without needing accounts or social features
